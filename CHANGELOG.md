# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-24

First release.

- `Z` to connect a node by searching for it, `Shift+Z` to jump to one.
- Pick the input with `Alt+A` (`B`, `A`, `mask`).
- Nodes you use a lot show up first. Roto and keyers show up first on the mask.
- `Alt+F` to look at a node before connecting, `Alt+P` to open its properties.
